# Confidentiality

You will often know more about our clients than they know about themselves. We process their most sensitive data, their strategies, and their secrets.

Because of this, we demand the highest standard of integrity. We do not talk about our clients outside of work. We do not share, write, or discuss their data with anyone-not friends, not family, and certainly not industry peers.

This extends to our own marketing. We do not use client logos or list them in our portfolio unless we have explicit written permission to do so.

We want confidentiality to be our competitive advantage, like a Swiss bank. We want the industry to know that InsCoder is a vault.

The goal isn't just to avoid a lawsuit; it is to build a reputation. We want your friends and family to know you as someone who never leaks a secret. When you treat client data with this level of respect, you elevate your job from a task to a true profession.

When someone asks you who we are working with or the details of a project, there is only one acceptable answer: 

**"I CANNOT TALK ABOUT IT."**
