# Credits

## 37signals

When we sat down to write our handbook, we started with the [37signals Employee Handbook](https://basecamp.com/handbook). We borrowed much of its structure and spirit, and adapted several sections for InsCoder. Our [page on moonlighting](moonlighting.md) is one example.

Their influence goes well beyond this handbook. You can see it in how we work remotely, why we write things down before calling a meeting, and how we give people room to manage their own work. Our eight-week work cycle grew out of ideas in *Shape Up*. We adjusted the timing to fit our team.

Four of their books helped shape our thinking:

- *REWORK*
- *REMOTE: Office Not Required*
- *It Doesn’t Have to Be Crazy at Work*
- *Shape Up*

We’ve also learned from their [Signal v. Noise essays](https://signalvnoise.com/) and [37signals on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@37signals/featured). We’re grateful to the people at 37signals for writing so openly about how they run their company. It gave us a place to start when we were figuring out how to run ours.
