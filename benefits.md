# Benefits

## Contract vs. Handbook Benefits

Your employment contract is the baseline. It sets out the company’s legal and contractual obligations to you.

This section describes our current benefits practices. These benefits are either equal to or more generous than what your contract requires. Some are intended to be permanent; others are experimental and may change over time.

We may amend, suspend, or discontinue handbook benefits. But we will not reduce anything expressly guaranteed in your employment contract unless both you and the company formally agree in writing.

## Paid Time Off

### Annual Leave

We offer 18 days of paid annual leave. We don’t carry leave over to the next year. If you leave the company, any accrued, unused days will be paid out prorated to your final day.

### Sabbatical

Every five years of continuous service, employees may take a six-week paid sabbatical. Give your team 3 to 6 months’ notice so they can coordinate; taking the leave is subject to our ability to successfully fill your role while you’re out. Sabbaticals do not accumulate-use it or lose it before your next five-year milestone. If you leave the company with an unused sabbatical, it is forfeited and will not be converted to a monetary equivalent.

### Sick Leave

If you’re sick, stop working. Your priority is to get better. Notify your manager and the rest of the company as soon as you can so we can adjust.

We typically pay for sick days. This is a discretionary act of trust, not a rigid rule. If we identify a pattern of abuse, we will revert to the strict terms of your employment contract.

### Early Leave on Designated Festivals

On designated festival eves, we finish early so you can start the celebration sooner. These are recurring annual events; you can find the schedule at this \[[**Link**](https://3.basecamp.com/3926929/buckets/6210089/messages/8119394231)\].

## Marriage Leave

Employees may take 5 consecutive days of paid leave to prepare for or celebrate their marriage. Use it within three months of your registration, before or after. The leave must be taken as a single, uninterrupted block; it cannot be split or used partially.

## Maternity Leave

If you are giving birth, we provide 16 weeks of maternity leave at 100% pay. 

You can start your leave 2 to 4 weeks before your due date in agreement with your manager; otherwise, it defaults to 4 weeks before. If you give birth earlier than planned, your leave starts on the day of delivery. Provide a medical certificate with your due date as soon as possible so we can coordinate.

Specific to Hong Kong: We bridge the government gap. The RMLP scheme \[[**Link**](https://www.rmlps.gov.hk/eng/home)\] covers weeks 11 to 14 but is capped at $80,000. InsCoder pays the difference to ensure you receive your full salary for the entire 16-week period.

## Bereavement Leave

When you lose a loved one, take the time you need to grieve and handle human family affairs. We offer up to 10 days of paid leave for the death of a spouse, parent, siblings or child. Notify your manager and the rest of the company as soon as you can so we can adjust. If your situation is complex (e.g., you were raised by your grandparents) and requires more time, talk to us.

## Medical Insurance

In Hong Kong, we provide medical insurance through Bowtie Life Insurance. If you are based elsewhere or prefer a different provider, you may arrange your own coverage. We will reimburse 100% of these payments, capped at the cost of the Hong Kong group plan.

Please note that Mainland China-based employees are covered by government-mandated social insurance (五險) and are therefore excluded from this additional private medical plan.

Reimbursable medical insurance must be a pure protection medical policy without any savings, investment, cash value, or refund-of-premium component. Policies that accumulate value, provide maturity/termination benefits, or combine with annuity or savings features are not eligible for reimbursement.

To be eligible, you must be the one paying for the policy personally. Because our Bowtie group plan renews once a year in early March, you must notify us by the end of February each year if you wish to cancel your Bowtie group coverage for the coming plan year. We process reimbursement claims for the previous year in March; simply include your total payments and supporting documents on your March invoice (or your final month's invoice if you leave mid‑year).

## Employee Profit Sharing

We share 10% of annual profits with the team. You become eligible after two years of service. Starting on your second anniversary, you accrue one share per month, maxing out at 10 years. We calculate shares monthly rather than annually to ensure fairness regardless of your hire date. Profits from the previous calendar year are distributed on or before the April salary payment of the following year (e.g., 2025 profits are paid by April 2026).

Think of this as a true bonus, not a guaranteed salary. Profits swing wildly; some years the payout will be significant, and other years there may be nothing to share. Do not budget for this money.

To receive a distribution, you must be employed at InsCoder on the payout date. We reserve the right to amend or cancel the program at any time.

## Retirement Plan

Hong Kong employees are enrolled in the MPF scheme, while Mainland China employees receive the Five Social Insurance and Housing Fund (五險一金).

If you are based elsewhere, InsCoder will match what you contribute to a retirement account in your home country, up to 5% of your basic salary capped at HK$1500/mo. In these cases, InsCoder will pay you directly via your monthly invoice. It’s up to you to invest that payment and maintain administration of the retirement account and its taxes. You may be asked to provide documentation to support your request for a match.
