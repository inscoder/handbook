# Equipment

We don’t do slow computers. To do your best work, you need tools that get out of the way.

## Standard Kit

Every staff member receives a kit designed for work efficiency:

* MacBook Pro  
* External Monitor (4K-5K, up to 27-inch)  
* Apple Keyboard & Magic Mouse\*

Our standard kit is a baseline, not a ceiling. Tools should remove friction, not add it. If you need specific hardware to do your best work, tell us. If it helps you get things done, we want you to have it.

## Sustainability & Waste

We do not treat hardware as disposable. We prioritize reusing high-quality gear that still runs like new. You might receive a spare unit we already have in stock, or we will buy new if needed. Regardless of the source, we guarantee the tool will handle your work without friction.

If you ever feel your gear is becoming a bottleneck, don’t suffer in silence. Flag it to your manager so we can fix it. 

## Software & Experiments

If you see a software, course, or subscription that will help you work smarter, let us know. If the cost is reasonable and it helps you build, we’ll likely fund it for everyone.

## Work Phones

If your role is client-facing (PM, Account Management, Sales), we’ll set you up with a dedicated phone and mobile plan. If your role evolves and you need one later, just ask.

*\* The Mouse Note: We know the Magic Mouse is controversial. If you prefer an ergonomic Logitech mouse, tell us. We’re happy to swap it out.*
