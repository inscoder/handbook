# Getting Started

Unless you’ve worked remotely before, InsCoder will feel different. There is no front desk to report to. You won't walk into a room full of new colleagues waiting to shake your hand one by one.

It will feel different-but we strive for a calm difference. Everyone at InsCoder is here to help you navigate Day 1 and beyond.

## Before You Start

A day or two before you start, your manager will email you instructions for your first day. This will include a specific schedule so you aren't guessing what to do next.

We will arrange the equipment you need to perform your work (MacBook, monitor, etc. - see the Equipment section for details). If you are in Hong Kong, you can pick this up at our office on your first day; otherwise, we will ship it directly to your home address.

## Your First Day

Your goal on Day 1 is to get connected with people, clear your admin to-dos, and participate in the onboarding training organized by your manager.

On your first day, you’ll log into Basecamp to see two projects dedicated to your onboarding:

* **Admin Stuff:** This contains to-dos to set up access to our internal systems and a few quick tutorials on Basecamp. You should aim to finish these tasks on your first day.

* **Onboarding Training:** This project contains content created by your manager to help you succeed in your role.

### Meeting the Team

While you tackle your to-dos, you will also have scheduled sessions to meet your support squad:

* **Your Manager:** For a 1:1 to align on priorities and early projects.

* **Your Team Buddy:** A colleague on your immediate team to help you with functional questions and getting set up.

* **Your InsCoder Buddy:** A friendly face not on your immediate team. They are here to help you settle into the culture.

We invest time in these introductions because we view employment as a partnership. We want you to build a successful career here, and that starts with knowing the people you work with.

## The First Few Weeks

Once your admin tasks are done, you will shift focus to your Onboarding Training. This usually takes about a few weeks.

If you are in Hong Kong, your team might ask you to work from the office for the first 1–2 weeks to get up to speed faster. If you are fully remote, we will simulate this support through frequent digital check-ins.

Eventually, you will wrap up the training and start working on real projects. We hope you have a wonderful journey with us.
