# Career Progression

## Promotions

We want you to advance. We actively look for opportunities to help you grow.

However, promotions are not automatic. Tenure does not equal entitlement. We debate before we commit. Every promotion must pass three questions:

* **Do we have the money?** If the business isn't growing, we can't expand payroll.  
* **Do we need the position?**  We don't create titles for the sake of progression. The role must be necessary.  
* **Are you ready?** Have you demonstrated the competence required for the new level?

Decisions follow review cycles, usually in March-April or September-October.

## Performance Review

We review performance twice a year.

* **Mid-Year (July/August):** A verbal check-in to ensure we are on the right track.

* **Full-Year (Jan/Feb):** A formal written review looking back at the whole year.

The goal is simple: show exactly how the company evaluates you, with zero ambiguity. We use this to drive promotion and pay raise decisions.

However, managers don't wait for performance review to address issues. They provide immediate feedback for on-the-spot course correction. Consequently, nothing in your formal review should ever come as a surprise.

## Pay

We are generous, but we do not compete to be the highest payer.

By default, everyone earns above the regional industry average for their role-typically 10–20% higher. We gauge this using real data from candidates during the hiring process. If you think our data is wrong, tell us.

### The Exception

For those who create unparalleled value, we aim to pay in the top 10% of the industry.

These people define us. They are Managers of One. They relentlessly drive the company forward, shape our culture, and work beyond their duties. Most importantly, they have the guts to lead us into uncharted waters when they see an opportunity.
