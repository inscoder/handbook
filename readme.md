# InsCoder Employee Handbook

People who work with us often say we’re different. They’re right.

It’s not an accident-it’s a choice. While other companies chase trends and complexity, we double down on the fundamentals the mainstream ignores. It doesn’t mean we’re smarter than anyone else. It just means we’ve decided to play a different game.

This handbook explains what that looks like in practice.

We wrote it to help you get settled from Day 1, so you aren't left guessing. Read it, use it, and you’ll see exactly why we work the way we do.
