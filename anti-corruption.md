# Anti-Corruption

## Read this. It keeps you out of jail.

Corruption is the one area where "smart" people ruin their lives because they think they can outmaneuver the system. They can't.

In Hong Kong, the law is simple: **You cannot take a secret cut.**

It is illegal to offer or accept any advantage related to your work without explicit permission. There are no gray areas here.

**The Three Rules**

1. **Don't take rewards.** Never accept a commission, rebate, or gift from a third party unless you have written permission from us.

2. **Don't give rewards.** Never offer a kickback to a client or partner unless they have written permission from their company to accept it.

3. **No fake schemes.** We don't touch fraudulent schemes. We don't help others hide money, and we don't forge documents to get government subsidies.

This is not a theoretical risk. The ICAC (Hong Kong's anti-corruption agency) frequently prosecutes people for exactly this. They arrest dozens of people at a time for using fake companies and forged invoices to defraud government funds. Do not get involved. Read the story here: \[[Link to Eng](https://www.icac.org.hk/en/p/press/index_id_2242.html) / [Link to Chi](https://www.icac.org.hk/tc/p/press/index_id_2242.html)\].

To understand the legal details, please read these FAQs from the ICAC: \[[Link to Eng](https://cpas.icac.hk/EN/Info/FAQ?cate_id=6)\] / \[[Link to Chi](https://cpas.icac.hk/ZH/Info/FAQ?cate_id=6)\].

## Shopify Commissions

Shopify pays partners a recurring commission for acquiring new clients. We accept this, but we make it transparent to our clients.

Whenever there is a possibility that our work could result in Shopify paying commissions to InsCoder, we let the client know and give them a choice:

1. Allow us to keep the commission.  
2. Ask us to donate the commission to a charity of their choice.

We are happy with either outcome. 

## The Sunshine Test

The best defense is the Sunshine Test. Use it:

*If the full details of this deal were published on the front page of the news, could we survive the scrutiny?*

If the deal relies on secrecy to work, it is almost certainly illegal. The answer is 'No'.

## If You Mess Up

Whether it was an honest mistake or a lapse in judgment, if you suspect you’ve crossed a line, tell us immediately.

Do not try to bury it. Buried problems don't die; they rot.

If you come forward now, we have a chance to resolve the situation legally. We can mitigate damage or correct the error before it spirals. But we can only help if we know.

Timing is the key. You want to handle this on your terms, not the authorities'. It is the difference between a difficult conversation with us and the police knocking on your door at 4:00 AM.
