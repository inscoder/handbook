# A Note About Moonlighting

Moonlighting means working other professional jobs outside of InsCoder.

We support side pursuits that help you grow or make life interesting. However, we must ensure these do not create a conflict of interest or drain the energy you owe to your work here. It is a delicate balance.

## What is OK

* Occasional Gigs: Helping a friend, a small design project, or a hobby business, provided it is small.

* Speaking: Occasional speaking gigs are fine. If it requires multiple days of travel, you must use your vacation time.

* Unrelated Business: Running a side business different from your day job (e.g., baking, photography), provided the commitment is low.

* Advisory Roles: Sitting on a board or advising a startup is fine if the time commitment is minimal (hours per month, not week).

## What is NOT OK

* Competitors: You cannot work full-time or part-time for another company or on your own behalf in our industry.

* Distraction: Taking on projects that require attention during business hours (e.g., launching an app that requires you to do customer support at 10 AM).

* Energy Drain: If you come to work exhausted because you are building a startup at night, you are failing to manage your energy.

* Aggressive Marketing: Actively marketing yourself for freelance work suggests your focus is not here.

## The Litmus Test

If you are unsure if your side project is a problem, ask yourself these four questions:

* Is it competitive with InsCoder?

* Does it occupy a disproportionate amount of your time?

* Does it require you to be away or distracted when you are needed at work?

* Is it another paid gig that employs the exact same skills we are paying you for?

In general, we want to be lenient within reason, but we will be strict if we see it impact your time, attention, or performance. It is hard to create one-size-fits-all rules. If you are in doubt, please reach out to your manager.
